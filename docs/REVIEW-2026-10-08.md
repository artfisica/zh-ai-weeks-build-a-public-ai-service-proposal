# Review and focused update — 8 October 2026

## The judgment

The project is worth pursuing because it makes a change of circumstances visible, reversible and discussable. The strongest moment is not a plausible AI answer. It is a resident changing one answer, seeing the consequences beside the previous path, and deciding what to keep. Preserve that as the product's centre.

The prototype can demonstrate that interaction. It cannot yet demonstrate a commune-maintained public service: the content is unreviewed, inference is variable, and a running Codespace is an operational dependency. Those are concrete pilot tasks, not reasons to discard the concept. The next step should establish one trusted journey, rather than widen the model's coverage faster than anyone can review it.

## What was actually reviewed

The supplied main-branch ZIP and the newer Claude `index.html` / `smoke.js` patch were inspected. Useful Claude changes were integrated: editable answered questions, typed alternatives, literal corrections and coverage reconsideration. The replacement does not simply apply a new coat of paint.

The existing deployed page was inspected in a real browser. The revised application was exercised with an isolated DOM/event/storage harness, its actual SVG output was rendered at desktop and mobile sizes, and selected real Swisscom/Apertus responses were sent through the updated application logic. Keep, Adopt and Undo are separate operations. Explore has its own story, answers and conversation. The last five accepted snapshots are retained, and the map cache is bounded. No proxy, key, provider configuration or presentation file is changed by this update.

## Evidence and limits

**44 dependency-free regression checks pass**, as does inline-script syntax validation. They cover state transitions, typed and clicked path changes, answered-question forks, correction, source filters, cancellation, reload, print contents, mobile/desktop SVG structure, labels, completion marks, focus and keyboard events. They test application behaviour; they do not certify administrative facts.

Real inference rehearsals produced a Nyon-family map and a Gland proposal/Keep. The answered C → B rehearsal initially exposed the model-response mismatch described above; after the repair, a new real response passed proposal, Adopt and Undo. Initial generation took approximately 25 seconds; the typed permit alternative took approximately 28 seconds across interpretation and generation. A later request failed at the network layer and retained the accepted map. Provider availability and latency are still variable. A later direct Gland rehearsal, seeded from a previous real initial response, exposed the structural loss described above. That failure is reproduced in the regression suite. After the continuity repair, a fresh direct Gland proposal passed: departure, tax and permit remained shared; arrival and school changed; Keep retained the accepted map. Its starting map was replayed from an earlier real response. Direct-call rehearsals are narrower checks than a fresh end-to-end browser session.

Do not describe a catalogue link as verified evidence for the generated action. All catalogue notes remain drafts, and the model can select an irrelevant page even from an allowed list. The place guard covers only a small named set of locations. A suggested office is not a certified competent authority. Dependencies are model-declared; preserving a completion mark is not an eligibility decision.

Do not promise arbitrary automatic understanding: a model may still misinterpret a follow-up. The underlined answers and editable sentence are the explicit correction route. Keep source gaps visible. A compact, useful map is preferable to a decorative abundance of uncertain obligations.

## What makes it educational and appealing

A resident should be able to explain three things after using it: what in their situation matters, what changes under an alternative, and where to check. Every question needs a reason. Every comparison needs a visible common part. Returning to the original must feel safe. Those properties make the map an instrument for learning, including for Swiss residents exploring another canton or language region.

The visual direction should stay quiet and legible: a sentence, a persistent map, a deliberate detail view. Animation should reveal the change rather than obscure it. Use fictional examples that invite exploration, not invented certainty. Professional export should carry unanswered questions as prominently as proposed actions.

The revised interface needs a real mobile/resident usability pass. SVG inspection cannot establish touch comfort, screen-reader quality or whether a first-time visitor understands the difference between Explore and Adopt.

## The project to ask Public AI to help start

1. **One journey, two communes:** choose a move involving Nyon and Saint-Cergue. Name an owner for each relevant claim, its scope, review date and contact. Store the pack separately from interface code.
2. **Conditions people can inspect:** turn reviewed consequential conditions into explicit rules. Let the model interpret language and explain the rules; do not let it silently rewrite them.
3. **Reliable access:** give the pilot a maintained inference endpoint and continuously available backend, with budget, operational ownership and honest failure behaviour.
4. **Retrieval with evaluation:** retrieve dated source passages, preserve publisher and jurisdiction, and test whether each passage supports its attached claim. Free crawling is not a substitute for review. Interviews in each community's language can capture practice and gaps; distinguish law, administrative procedure and lived experience before publishing.
5. **Resident and office testing:** include residents arriving from abroad and residents moving within Switzerland. Test whether they can revise an answer, understand the difference and bring an annotated plan to an appropriate office or adviser.
6. **Model comparison later:** evaluate two models against the same reviewed pack and scenarios before adding a public selector. Agreement between models does not establish a fact; disagreements should help reviewers locate uncertainty. Switching models must create a separate version rather than silently changing a saved path.

The six-week pilot in `PILOT.md` is the right sized request: a technical counterpart, access to inference, introductions to content owners, and a reviewable result. Success is a small journey that residents and officials can inspect and correct. A beautiful demo opens that conversation; maintaining trustworthy local content is what makes it a public service.
