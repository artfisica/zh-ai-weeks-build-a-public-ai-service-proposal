# Deep PR review — 25 October 2026

## Result and scope

The four open PRs were reviewed individually and combined in a fresh temporary checkout. The review found real gaps beyond the first green test run and corrected them in the PR branches. The review covers application state, model-output handling, source provenance, cost controls, Codespaces startup, browser interaction, print output and development checks. `main` and the deployed demo were not changed by this work.

This is your personal prototype preparing for the Public AI follow-up. The review does not introduce a consultancy engagement, hosting migration or municipal approval process.

## What this review cannot prove

- The model can still misunderstand a situation or attach an allowed source to an unsupported action. The ten live fictional cases must be inspected for that. The earlier 404 was consistent with a stopped Codespace, not evidence that the demo application was broken; start it for the live rehearsal.
- Catalogue notes remain drafts. Review metadata and source records preserve provenance; they do not authenticate a reviewer or certify a generated step.
- Automated accessibility checks and engine emulation do not establish comfort on a physical phone or a successful screen-reader session. Focus movement and the meaning of Explore/Adopt should be observed with people.
- The file-backed budget supports one proxy process. Multiple replicas need a transactional shared counter. The container is an optional deployment artifact; no host or provider account was changed.
